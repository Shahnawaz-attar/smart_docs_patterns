# Company Policies 2026

## Remote Work Policy
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days a week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are mandatory office days for team collaboration.

## Leave Policy
- Paid Time Off (PTO): 20 days per year
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Maternity Leave: 6 months fully paid
- Paternity Leave: 2 months fully paid

## Reimbursement
Employees can claim up to $1000 per year for learning and development courses. Internet and home office equipment claims are capped at $500 per year.
